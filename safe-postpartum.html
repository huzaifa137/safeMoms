Newborn and mother care

Support through the fourth trimester and beyond.

SafeMom provides comprehensive postpartum support for mothers and newborns, with continuous guidance, monitoring and education that help families thrive.

Through trained youth navigators, home visits and digital tools, mothers are never alone after childbirth.

Holistic postpartum services

Comprehensive mother and newborn care.

Community-led support and digital health innovation address the physical, emotional and developmental needs of mothers and babies.

Newborn health checks

Continuous monitoring of growth, feeding patterns and developmental milestones, with early detection of concerns.

  • Growth monitoring
  • Feeding assessment
  • Developmental milestone checks

Breastfeeding support

Expert guidance on latching, feeding schedules and common challenges, supporting nutrition and mother-baby bonding.

  • Latching techniques
  • Feeding schedules
  • Solutions to common challenges

Maternal mental health

Early detection of postpartum depression and anxiety using validated tools, with specialist referrals.

  • Depression screening
  • Anxiety assessment
  • Specialist referrals

Immunisation support

SMS reminders and scheduling help so infants receive vaccinations on time.

  • SMS reminders
  • Vaccination schedule planning
  • Follow-up support

Mother recovery care

Guidance on physical recovery, wound care and nutrition, with personalised plans for healing.

  • Wound care
  • Nutrition counselling
  • Recovery tracking

Family planning

Counselling and support for postpartum contraception and informed birth spacing.

  • Contraception options
  • Spacing guidance
  • Informed decision support
Your 6-week postpartum journey

Structured support at every stage.

DAYS 1-21

Initial recovery phase

Post-birth care education covering cord care, feeding guidance and warning signs, plus mental health assessment and early intervention.

WEEKS 1-4

Regular home visits

Baby weight checks and growth monitoring, feeding evaluation and breastfeeding support, mental health screening and counselling.

WEEKS 4-6+

Long-term wellness

Postpartum check-ups and follow-up appointments, vaccination milestone tracking and reminders, and family planning counselling.

Mental health matters

Supporting a mother’s wellbeing.

Postpartum depression affects roughly 1 in 7 mothers, yet often goes undiagnosed. Our navigators are trained to recognise early warning signs and offer compassionate support.

Regular screening

Validated screening tools during home visits.

Specialist referrals

Pathways to mental health professionals.

Peer support

Mother-to-mother support groups.

Family education

Helping family members recognise warning signs.
