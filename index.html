Who we are

No mother should walk her journey alone.

At the heart of SafeMom is a growing network of trained youth navigators: passionate young people who serve as trusted companions for mothers in their own communities.

Through home visits, digital reminders, emergency transport coordination and compassionate guidance, we make sure mothers receive timely, respectful and lifesaving care, right from where they live.

About SafeMom
What we offer

Three stages of care, from first trimester to one year postpartum.

SafeMom supports mothers through the whole maternal journey, so care never stops at the clinic door.

Stage 1

SafePregnancy

Appointment reminders, home visits, health education and counselling from early pregnancy through birth planning.

Learn more
Stage 2

SafeDelivery

A 24/7 helpline, transport support, emergency referral and a birth companion, so mothers reach skilled care safely and on time.

Learn more
Stage 3

SafePostpartum

Postpartum follow-up, breastfeeding support, mental wellbeing, family planning and progress tracking for mother and baby.

Our mission

What we do

To empower communities with the knowledge, tools and support needed to ensure safe pregnancy, safe delivery and safe postpartum care for every mother, starting at the household level.

Our vision

What we hope for

A Uganda where no mother dies while giving life, and where every woman, regardless of location, income or background, receives timely, compassionate and high-quality maternal health support.

How it works

Getting support is simple.

Four steps, free of charge, and no documents are needed to register.

Register

Call our helpline, visit a local health centre or ask a community health worker to register you.

Meet your navigator

A trained youth navigator from your community is matched to you and stays with you.

Visits and reminders

Home visits, health education and SMS reminders for every check-up, from pregnancy to recovery.

Help when it matters

If danger signs appear, your navigator coordinates emergency transport and alerts the facility.

Why these numbers matter

Our work is rooted in community, powered by youth and strengthened by data.

  • More mothers are accessing timely antenatal and postnatal care
  • More families are prepared for childbirth
  • More emergencies are handled quickly
  • More young people are employed and empowered
  • More communities are becoming safer for mothers

Impact data is updated quarterly in collaboration with our community health partners.
