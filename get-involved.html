Become a youth navigator

Lead change in your community.

Join a growing network of trained young leaders who provide essential maternal health support, while gaining valuable skills and experience.

What you will do

  • Conduct home visits to expectant and new mothers
  • Provide health education on prenatal, delivery and postpartum care
  • Accompany mothers as a birth companion
  • Coordinate emergency transport and referrals

Who can apply: young people aged 18 to 30 with a secondary education, a passion for maternal health and a commitment to serving their community.

Phase 1

Core training

  • Maternal health basics
  • Danger signs recognition
  • Communication skills
  • Community engagement
Phase 2

Field practicum

  • Supervised home visits
  • Case management
  • Emergency simulation
  • Mentorship sessions

On completion you receive a certification as a Maternal Health Navigator, with ongoing mentorship and peer support groups. Training is free.
