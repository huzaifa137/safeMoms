Partnership models

Frameworks tailored to your goals.

Together we can create sustainable impact, strengthen health systems and save more mothers and babies.

Healthcare facilities

Hospitals, clinics and health centres partnering for referrals and integrated care.

  • Streamlined patient referrals
  • Joint training programs
  • Data sharing for continuum of care

Corporate partners

Businesses supporting maternal health through CSR, employee engagement and funding.

  • CSR program development
  • Employee volunteer programs
  • Strategic funding opportunities

NGO and community organisations

Local organisations working together to strengthen community health systems.

  • Joint program implementation
  • Capacity building workshops
  • Resource sharing and optimisation
Research and academic institutions

Evidence that improves care.

Universities and research organisations collaborating on maternal health studies, program evaluation and innovation development.
