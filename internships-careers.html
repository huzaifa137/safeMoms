Internships and careers.

Learn, grow and help make pregnancy and birth safer for every mother in Uganda.

Partner with usVolunteerInternships and Careers

Work with us

Build a career in maternal health.

SafeMom is a young, fast-learning organisation led by young people. We offer internships and, as we grow, paid roles for people who care about safer pregnancies and births.

We welcome students, graduates and early-career professionals from health, social sciences, technology, communications and business backgrounds.

Internships

Learn by doing, alongside a supportive team.

Placements are shaped around your studies and our current needs.

Community health and outreach

Support navigators, home visits and community health education.

  • Field exposure
  • Mentorship from experienced staff

Monitoring, evaluation and research

Help collect and analyse program data and prepare reports.

  • Data and reporting skills
  • Contribute to publications

Communications and fundraising

Create stories, content and campaigns that bring mothers’ experiences to a wider audience.

  • Writing, photo and video
  • Donor and partner outreach

Digital health and technology

Support our SMS reminder and referral tools.

  • Systems and data support
  • Low-tech innovation experience
