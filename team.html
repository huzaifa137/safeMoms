The board, leaders and navigators behind SafeMom, and how we stay accountable.

Who we areOur approachWhere we workTeam and governance

Governance

Oversight, accountability and openness.

Strong governance is how we earn and keep the trust of mothers, communities and partners.

Board oversight

Our Board provides governance and accountability, with expertise in public health, maternal care, youth development and digital innovation.

Data and reporting

Impact data is updated quarterly with community health partners, and reports are published openly.

Mothers first

Services are free, and information about mothers and families is kept confidential.
