Volunteer opportunities

Share your skills, transform lives.

Whether you have a few hours or regular time to give, your contribution matters.

Community outreach

  • Community engagement
  • Local languages

Content creation

Create educational materials, social media content and awareness campaigns about maternal health. Remote, flexible.

  • Writing
  • Graphic design
  • Video editing

Counselling support

  • Active listening
  • Empathy
  • Psychology background

Data analysis

  • Excel
  • Statistics
  • Reporting

Volunteer FAQs

How much time do I need to commit?

We offer flexible opportunities ranging from 2 to 20 hours per week. Most roles ask for a minimum of 3 months, with options for short-term projects.

Do I need specific qualifications?

Most roles do not. Passion and commitment matter most. Specialised roles state any experience needed.

Can I volunteer remotely?

Yes. Content creation, data analysis, virtual counselling and social media are all remote options.

What support will I receive?

Training, regular check-ins with a supervisor, access to resources and materials, and opportunities for skill development and networking.
