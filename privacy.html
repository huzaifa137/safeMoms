Privacy Policy

Your trust matters. Here is how we handle your information.

Draft template. Have this reviewed and adapted to local law before publishing.

Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to give us through forms, calls or visits, such as your name, contact details and the reason you are reaching out.

How we use it

We use information to respond to you, provide support, run our programs and report anonymously on our impact. We do not sell personal data.

Confidentiality

Information about mothers and families is kept confidential. We share it only with your consent, or where required by law or to protect someone from serious harm, for example to arrange emergency care.

Security and retention

We store information securely and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes described above.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by contacting hello@safemomug.org.
